Bronze Age Timeline

  1. 3000 BCE

    Early Cycladic cultures develop in the Aegean

  2. 2000 BCE

    Minoan civilization flourishes on Crete

  3. 1900-1700 BCE

    Major Minoan palace centers develop, including Knossos

  4. 1600 BCE

    Mycenaean civilization becomes prominent on mainland Greece

  5. 1450 BCE

    Mycenaeans take control of major centers in Crete

  6. 1400-1200 BCE

    Mycenaean civilization reaches its height

  7. 1250 BCE

    Mycenae's famous Lion Gate and massive fortifications are in use

  8. 1200 BCE

    Major Bronze Age civilizations across the eastern Mediterranean experience widespread disruption

  9. 1100 BCE

    Mycenaean palace civilization collapses; the Greek Dark Age begins
