What is Phlosophy?

Philosophy comes from the Ancient Greek word φιλοσοφία (philosophia), meaning "love of wisdom". It is the study of fundamental questions about life, reality, knowledge, reason, and human behaviour. Philosophers try to understand how the world works, what it means to live a good life, and how people can distinguish truth from falsehood

In Ancient Greece, philosophy was not just a school of subject, but a way of thinking about the world and our place in it. Greek philosophers asked big questions such as: What is real? What is knowledge? What is the good life? What is the nature of justice? Their ideas laid the foundation for many fields we still study today, including ethics, politics, science, and logic.

"The unexamined life is not worth of living"

-Socrates
