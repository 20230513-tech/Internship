Did You Know?

1. Olympics

The first recorded Olympic Games took place in 776 BC in Olympia. They were held in honor of Zeus.

2. Socrates Wrote Nothing

Socrates did not leave behind any writings of his own. Much of what we know about his ideas comes from writers such as Plato.

3. Ancient Greek Alarm Clock

The ancient engineer Ctesibius developed water clocks, and ancient Greek engineers experimented with devices that could produce sounds at predetermined times.
